# Omuse 0.3.0 — Crop, zoom and editable clipboard

**30 September 2026 · Arch Linux / Omarchy · Linux x86_64 source pre-release**

This release improves everyday editing: frame a photo before cropping, inspect
detail without losing your place, and carry editable artwork between projects.
Project formats and provider connections are unchanged.

## Frame the image before cropping

Press **C** to preview a crop. Choose Free, Original, 1:1, 4:5, 3:2 or 16:9;
**Swap** reverses the orientation. Drag a corner to resize or drag inside to
move the frame. Arrow keys nudge by one pixel; Shift nudges by ten.

**Enter / Apply** commits one undo step. **Escape / Cancel** leaves the artwork
unchanged. An existing pixel selection seeds the frame. Source pixels outside
the new canvas remain retained, and Undo restores the previous canvas. Fixed
ratios can round by one pixel at integer output sizes.

## Copy editable layers and groups

Clear the pixel selection with **Ctrl+D**, select the layers or group, and use
**Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V**. Within the same running Omuse process, groups, live type,
shapes, masks, filters, transforms, locks and retained sources stay editable.
Paste assigns fresh identities and remaps internal mask links in one undo step.
Copy the parent group when its context contributes to an isolated child.

Other applications receive PNG. Editable copying is bounded to **256 MiB** of
retained content and a **16 MP source canvas** for its full-resolution PNG.
Missing live-mask dependencies or oversized content are refused before editing.
Separate processes, app restarts and clipboard-manager replay do not provide
editable layer transport. The [photo guide](https://github.com/Sugata-Software/Omuse/blob/main/docs/user-guide/photo-editing.md#copy-editable-artwork-between-documents)
explains pixel selections, complete-layer copying and large-document alternatives.

## Keep your place while zooming

**Ctrl+= / Ctrl+-** use fixed zoom stops from 2% to 1600%, keeping the artwork
point at the viewport centre fixed. **Ctrl+1** preserves that anchor at actual
pixels. Scrolling zooms around the pointer; **Ctrl+0** fits and recentres.

## Reliability fixes

- Wayland and XWayland now publish image clipboard formats correctly.
- Wayland transfers use bounded nonblocking writes, preserving progress through
  partial writes and yielding to the UI when a reader is slow.
- Delayed Paste refuses stale document, selection, layer or clipboard state and
  cannot interrupt an active crop, gesture or dialog.
- Pixel Cut refuses masks, opacity, live appearances or resampling that would
  omit source content from the clipboard. Refused and no-op pixel/mask cuts
  preserve the previous clipboard and artwork. Whole-layer Cut remains available
  when its layers are unlocked and dependencies allow removal.

## Current limits

This remains a source pre-release with no application binary attached.
Clean-target installation, broader physical input/accessibility coverage,
independent photo/interchange corpora and dependency-notice gates remain open.
The clipboard writer bounds callback work; an idle reader can retain its
transfer buffer until it closes.
